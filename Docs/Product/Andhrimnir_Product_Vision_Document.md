# Andhrímnir Product Vision Document

Product Vision Document

Andhrímnir – AI Incident Intelligence Platform for Restaurants

## Executive Summary

Restaurants generate large amounts of operational data through orders, inventory, reservations, kitchen workflows, and staff activities. However, most existing software records these activities rather than interpreting them. Managers often discover operational failures only after customers are affected.

Andhrímnir is an AI Incident Intelligence Platform that transforms operational signals into actionable intelligence. Instead of replacing POS or restaurant management systems, it functions as an intelligence layer that detects incidents, explains their causes, and recommends timely actions that improve operational efficiency and customer experience.

## Why Now?

Artificial intelligence has become accessible enough to augment operational decision-making, while modern restaurants already generate digital operational data. At the same time, customer expectations for speed and service continue to increase and restaurant profit margins remain thin. Small operational improvements can therefore produce significant business impact.

## Problem Landscape

A typical dinner service can deteriorate quickly: a delayed kitchen station causes order backlogs, ingredients run out unexpectedly, waiting times increase, and customer satisfaction declines. Existing systems provide operational records but rarely provide operational intelligence before these issues escalate.

Restaurants need proactive awareness instead of reactive reporting.

## Vision Statement

Empower every restaurant to operate proactively through AI-driven operational intelligence that identifies, explains, and helps resolve operational disruptions before they impact customers and business performance.

## Mission Statement

Build an operational intelligence platform that continuously analyzes restaurant activities, detects operational incidents, provides contextual recommendations, and enables restaurant teams to make informed decisions in real time.

## How It Works

1. Collect operational data from restaurant workflows.
2. Evaluate operational rules to detect incidents.
3. Build operational context.
4. Generate AI-assisted recommendations.
5. Present recommendations through the management dashboard.
6. Managers make informed operational decisions.

## AI Intelligence Layer

The platform combines deterministic operational rules with an AI intelligence layer. Rules identify operational incidents consistently, while AI interprets the broader operational context and generates explainable recommendations. Initial MVP use cases include kitchen bottleneck detection, inventory shortage alerts, and table congestion management.

## Value Proposition

Restaurants lose revenue because operations fail—not because transactions fail. Andhrímnir improves operational visibility, reduces delays, minimizes food waste, improves staff productivity, and enables faster managerial response.
